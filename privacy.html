AutoKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AutoKeep is a vehicle maintenance and service tracking application published by JPV Apps. This policy explains how information is handled when using AutoKeep.

1. Vehicle information

Users can enter vehicle names, registration numbers, mileage, service records, dates, maintenance descriptions and costs. This information is stored locally on the user's device using application storage. AutoKeep does not require an online account to manage these records.

2. Payments

AutoKeep may offer optional paid features through Google Play Billing. Purchases are processed by Google Play. AutoKeep may receive purchase status and transaction identifiers needed to provide purchased features. Payment card details are handled by Google, not directly by AutoKeep.

3. Sharing information

AutoKeep does not intentionally transmit locally stored vehicle and service records to the developer's servers. Users may choose to copy, export or share their records using features provided by the application or device.

4. Data retention and deletion

Vehicle records remain in local application storage until they are edited, deleted, or the application's stored data is cleared. Uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored records. Users should keep their own backups before clearing data or uninstalling.

5. Third-party services

Google Play services may process information necessary for app distribution, purchases, security and related services according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

6. Security

AutoKeep uses device application storage for vehicle records. Users should protect access to their devices and maintain backups of important information. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

AutoKeep is intended for general vehicle owners and is not specifically designed for children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AutoKeep's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact JPV Apps at workshopmanager.support@gmail.com.
